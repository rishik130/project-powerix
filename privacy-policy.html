Your privacy matters to Powerix. This Privacy Policy explains how Powerix Energy Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Powerix", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information obtained from users ("you") of our website and services. By accessing or using our platform, you agree to the practices described in this document.
01
📋

Introduction

Powerix is a renewable energy services company operating in India, specializing in Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), grid approvals, transaction management, regulatory compliance, and carbon credit advisory services. We are committed to maintaining the trust and confidence of all visitors to our website and clients of our services.

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our website (www.powerix.in), mobile applications, and any related services, sales, marketing, or events. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our services.

02
📥

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users and clients. The types of information we collect include:

Category Examples How Collected
Personal Identification Name, email address, phone number, company name Contact forms, registration, enquiries
Business Information Project details, energy capacity, site location, billing data Service onboarding, PPA consultations
Technical Data IP address, browser type, device ID, pages visited Automatically via cookies & analytics
Communication Data Emails, chat transcripts, call records (with consent) Direct communication with our team
Financial Data Bank details, invoice records (for settlement purposes) Transaction management services
Note: We do not collect sensitive personal data such as biometric information, religious beliefs, or health data. Financial data is only collected when strictly necessary for our transaction management services and is handled with the highest level of security.
03
⚙️

How We Use Your Information

Powerix uses the information collected for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide and manage our renewable energy services including PPA structuring, approval support, and carbon credit advisory.
  • Communication: To respond to enquiries, send service updates, consultation confirmations, and relevant notifications.
  • Compliance: To meet legal and regulatory obligations under Indian laws including the IT Act, 2000 and applicable energy regulations.
  • Improvement: To analyse usage patterns, improve our website experience, and develop new service offerings.
  • Marketing: To send promotional materials, newsletters, and updates about our services — only with your explicit consent.
  • Transaction Processing: To process energy allocation settlements, payment recoveries, and banking agreements on your behalf.
  • Security: To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activities.
04
🤝

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Regulatory Authorities: Government bodies, electricity regulatory commissions (CERC, SERCs), and grid operators as required for approvals and compliance.
  • Service Partners: Third-party auditors, verification agencies, and carbon registry bodies involved in delivering our services (e.g., Verra, Gold Standard).
  • Legal Obligations: When required by law, court order, or government authority under applicable Indian legislation.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, with prior notice.
  • With Your Consent: Any other sharing of your information will only occur with your explicit consent.
We require all third parties to respect the security of your personal data and to treat it in accordance with applicable data protection laws. We do not allow third-party service providers to use your personal data for their own purposes.
05
🍪

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyse site traffic. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., Google Analytics). All data is anonymised.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences for a better experience on return visits.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements. These are only set with your consent.

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

06
🔒

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security practices include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted through our website
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure cloud infrastructure with access controls and audit logs
  • Employee training on data protection and privacy best practices
Important: While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. If you have reason to believe your interaction with us is no longer secure, please contact us immediately at privacy@powerix.in.
07
🗂️

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Client Records: Retained for 7 years following the end of our service engagement, in accordance with Indian accounting and tax regulations.
  • Enquiry / Contact Data: Retained for 2 years from the date of last interaction.
  • Website Analytics: Aggregated data retained indefinitely; identifiable data deleted after 26 months.
  • Marketing Preferences: Retained until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.

Upon expiry of the retention period, your data will be securely deleted or anonymised so that it can no longer be associated with you.

08
⚖️

Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection laws (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  1. Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  2. Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  3. Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  4. Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for processing at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  5. Right to Grievance Redressal: Lodge a complaint with our Data Protection Officer or the relevant supervisory authority.
  6. Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@powerix.in. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business professionals and are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a person under 18, we will take prompt steps to delete such information from our records.

If you believe we have collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately at privacy@powerix.in.
